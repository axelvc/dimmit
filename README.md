## Why Dimmit?

Most external monitors don't sync brightness with your Mac. Dimmit fixes that. It talks to your displays through DDC, adjusting hardware brightness and contrast so your eyes stay comfortable without reaching for the monitor buttons.

## Features

- **Hardware control** — Adjust brightness and contrast directly in your monitor's hardware via DDC/CI.
- **Menu bar panel** — A compact panel that sits in your menu bar, ready when you need it.
- **Clear status** — See which displays are connected and when DDC control is available.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** — Adjust brightness without leaving your keyboard.
- **Brightness HUD** — Visual feedback as you make changes.
- **Native to macOS** — Familiar typography, controls, and patterns that feel like part of your Mac.

## Requirements

- macOS 26 or later
- An external display that supports DDC/CI

## How it works

1. Download and open Dimmit.
2. It appears in your menu bar with a panel showing all connected displays.
3. Drag the sliders to adjust brightness and contrast — changes happen instantly on your monitor.
